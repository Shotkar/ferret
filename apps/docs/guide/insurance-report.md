# Insurance report

**Insurance report** (in the menu) is the list an insurer or adjuster asks for after a loss: every item with where it
is, what it is (make, model, serial number), how many, its condition, what you paid, what it's worth, its photos and
receipts, and its warranty — grouped, with totals. Pick one property or all of them.

![The insurance report, ready to print or save as a PDF](/screenshots/report-light.webp){.shot .wide}

- **Print or save as PDF** (on an iPhone: Share → Print, then Share again → Save to Files).
- **Download spreadsheet (CSV)** for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Amounts are plain numbers (so they add up), with a
  **Currency** column saying what they're in.

Boxes aren't listed (they're packaging), unless you gave one a value — a jewelry case, say.

## Ready for a claim?

The home screen shows what's missing before you'd need to claim: things without a **photo**, a **value**, a
**place**, a **document** (a receipt) or a **category**, and valuable things without a **serial number**. Each opens
the list of items missing it.

A thing's **replacement cost** (what a new one would cost, which you set, maybe from the AI's
[estimate](./ai#replacement-cost)) shows beside its price and in the spreadsheet.
