# Warranties and claims

## Warranties

On an item's page, add a warranty: when it ends (and starts), who provides it, what it covers, and the document it's
on. Ferret can suggest one when it reads a warranty card or a receipt — tap it to add it.

The home screen shows warranties ending soon, and the bell lists those ending within a month (see
[Needs attention](./attention)). Within a week, the daily reminder mentions them too.

**Warranties** lists every warranty in the household. Open it from the home screen's Warranties card, or from
Insurance in the menu (**More** on a phone). Claims still in progress come first, then warranties ending in the next
60 days, then the ones still covered. Ended warranties are folded away at the bottom. Tap one to open its item, where
you change it or file a claim.

## Return windows

When a store only takes something back for a while, give the item a **Return by** date (**Edit** → *Return by*). Its
page shows how many days are left, and the bell says so the week before — urgently, so in the
[daily reminder](./reminders) too, for the last two days. After that day, or once the thing is
[gone](./past-things), it isn't mentioned again.

Ferret fills it in for you in two ways, never without asking:

- **From a receipt**: when it reads a return period (*returns within 30 days*) or a printed return date, it's offered
  with the other suggestions on the item's page — tap **Apply**.
- **From an Amazon import**: orders from the last 30 days get a return-by date 30 days after the order date (Amazon's
  usual window; the download doesn't say when things arrived). Change it if yours is different.

## Claims

When something breaks under warranty, file a **claim** under that warranty: what went wrong, the provider's claim or
case number, and how it went — **filed → in progress → approved or denied → resolved**, with what it cost you (a
deductible, shipping). A claim with no news for two weeks shows up in the bell as a nudge to follow up.
